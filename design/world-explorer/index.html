About this prototype and its coverage

This uses the existing collection, without adding historical claims. Matches come from recorded reference points or dated place associations overlapping the selected years. They don't establish the full extent of an event, state or culture.

Entries without suitable locations may be missing. An empty timeline means we haven't matched records to this area and period, not that nothing happened there. Country outlines are the existing Natural Earth dataset, not a statement about disputed borders.

Open a history to use the existing explorer. Your browser's Back button returns to this selection. A dedicated return link is part of the implementation plan.
