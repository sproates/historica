## Additional source inspection

The reviewer opened the Ministry's Mari urban-stratigraphy and palace-excavation pages, UNESCO's Babylon record and Iraq's Nippur tentative-list submission, and the transcript of Susanne Paulus's interview. These provide the additional chronology and site references. Sources and passage locators are recorded against the individual claims in the reviewed package. School interpretation and curriculum are separate claims. References to exact excavation dates were omitted as unnecessary here.

For Mari, Getty TGN 6003722 was rejected as a coordinate source: its point (36.4667 N, 37.1833 E) does not match the Euphrates location described by the archaeological mission and UNESCO. The inspected Interactive Archaeological Map entry supplies 34.5494 N, 40.89 E and identifies Tell Hariri northwest of Abu Kamal. This is a secondary site's reference point, not a surveyed palace coordinate; that limitation is retained. An inspected Gallet et al. 2020 accepted manuscript independently uses Mari latitude 34.55 N but does not supply the full point used here. It was not adopted as the coordinate citation. Pleiades could not be inspected because of its access challenge. No source image or full paper has been added to the repository.

The source mission uses circa 1760 BCE for the end of City III, while the pilot's Ministry glossary used 1761 in its military narrative. The entry explicitly follows the phase chronology and does not assert an exact attack year. The original pilot's question about city versus palace destruction is resolved for the retained palace claim by the mission's explicit palace account.
