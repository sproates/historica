# Qin and Han expansion

Nine connected entries extend the existing Chinese chronology: the First Emperor, Qin imperial rule and unification, the mausoleum and terracotta army, Chang’an, Sima Qian’s historical writing, Ban Chao and Luoyang.

Sources: the Met’s Qin and Han essays and UNESCO’s mausoleum description and geographical component table. Public passages inspected on 22 November 2026; this is source checking, not independent specialist review or a completed regional research package.

The mausoleum and army use separate UNESCO component coordinates. The First Emperor has a burial association only in 210 BCE, not an inferred itinerary. Cities use explicit dynastic study windows; the Shiji entry uses Wu’s reign as context rather than asserting composition dates. The terracotta army uses the wider mausoleum construction window, not precise statue production dates. We do not adopt the Met Qin essay’s conflicting accession age or UNESCO’s Sima Qian lifespan. No modern national borders are projected onto ancient unification.

Connections to the Mauryas indicate contemporaneity only. Further work should cover early Han succession, Mawangdui and ordinary life, and independently sourced Central Asian journeys. City coordinates remain unchecked and are not invented.
