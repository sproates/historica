# Making the existing collection more useful

Priority is the journeys visitors can make, rather than adding regions for their own sake.

## First pass: 23 November 2026

The initial inventory found 43 entities without explicit relationship rows. That was not a count of visitor dead ends: the model also derives links from geographic associations and campaign-specific rules. Checking the rendered relationship model revealed 11 actual dead ends. Nineteen explicit, reciprocal connections now give every existing focus at least one onward link to a different entry and expose selected everyday-life entries from broader periods.

These links organise existing sourced records; they do not assert new personal encounters or diplomatic contact. The new adapter records both endpoint source URLs. The pair comparing Qin imperial rule and Chu silk is explicitly a temporal comparison.

Memphis now uses UNESCO component 86-001. Egyptian Thebes uses the Karnak component 087-001 as an explicitly labelled reference within the larger city. These are points, not territorial outlines, ancient city centres or evidence of a person's presence. Coordinate evidence is added to the Egyptian evidence pack without rewriting existing accepted claims.

## Remaining priorities

1. Compare lives: add sourced biographies, including women, where birth and death evidence allows meaningful age comparisons. Keep reigns separate when life dates are unknown. Approximate birth dates must produce approximate ages.
2. Locate people in time: record documented dated visits or residence separately from broad affiliations. Check Pataliputra, Han Chang’an and Han Luoyang references. Geographic overview records do not need arbitrary central points.
3. Follow ordinary lives: deepen work, households, food, trade and belief with dated, located archaeological evidence. Connect these from rulers and periods without implying personal involvement.
4. Inspect uncertainty: audit broad study windows versus event dates, lifespans and reigns, particularly where the same underlying subject has multiple entries.
5. Historical areas: source reusable geometry with date ranges and explicit limits. Do not derive ancient territory from modern country polygons.

This release changes data and generated pages only. New comparison controls, relationship presentation or map interaction would require a local design preview before publication.
