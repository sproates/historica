Lumbini is a Buddhist pilgrimage site in what is now southern Nepal. Its archaeological remains include Ashoka’s inscribed pillar and structures dating back to the third century BCE.

The pillar connects the place to a recorded royal visit, alongside the longer history of worship at the site.
