Pericles was an Athenian politician and commander, born around 495 BCE. His career belonged to the period when Athens became a dominant power in the Greek world.

He was still leading the city at the beginning of the Peloponnesian War. He died during the epidemic in 429 BCE, early in a conflict that would continue for another quarter of a century.
