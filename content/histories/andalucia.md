Explore ancient places in what is now Andalucía: the houses of Phoenician Gadir, the fish-processing town of Baelo Claudia and the Roman city of Itálica. They offer different views of settlement, work and urban life in southern Spain.

Andalucía is used here as a modern geographical grouping. These places did not all belong to one ancient state, and the selected entries cover only part of the region’s history.
