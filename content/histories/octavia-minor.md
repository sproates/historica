Octavia was Augustus’ sister and married Mark Antony. She tried to mediate between the two men as their alliance deteriorated.

After Antony’s death, she brought up children from his marriages and his relationship with Cleopatra alongside her own family. Her household became part of the dynasty Augustus was building.
