Just before the fleet sailed for Sicily, sacred stone figures called herms were mutilated across Athens. The attack provoked an investigation into sacrilege.

Alcibiades became caught up in accusations concerning religious offences. Accusations against him should not be read as proof that he personally damaged the herms.
