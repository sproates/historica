Lahun lies at the entrance to the Fayum. Its ancient town, often called Kahun, preserves evidence of life in the late Middle Kingdom, roughly 1850–1700 BCE.

Papyri, furniture and agricultural tools survive alongside the town plan. Together they provide a way into work and administration that royal monuments alone cannot offer. There is a limitation, though: the site was cleared in 1889, when most objects were not recorded with the precise findspots archaeologists would expect today.
