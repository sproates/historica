A limestone writing surface from Deir el-Medina lists forty workers and records reasons for absence. They include illness, deaths in the family, house building and work for a superior.

This was an administrative record, not a survey of everyone’s working life. It gives individual glimpses of the obligations and interruptions behind the royal tombs.
