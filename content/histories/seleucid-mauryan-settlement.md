Chandragupta and Seleucus reached a settlement after conflict near the Indus. Their agreement connected the Mauryan state with one of the kingdoms formed after Alexander’s death.

The early evidence does not clearly establish who won the fighting. Later accounts have turned that uncertainty into very different stories.
