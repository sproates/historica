El Romeral uses a different structural approach from Menga and Viera. Its passage leads into corbelled chambers, with walls that narrow upwards to form the roofs.

Its construction date is less secure. The 2016 ICOMOS assessment assigns it broadly to the Copper Age, around 3300–2200 BCE, while acknowledging that the detailed chronology remains uncertain. The long bar on the timeline represents that uncertainty, not a thousand-year building project.
