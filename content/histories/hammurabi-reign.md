Hammurabi ruled Babylon around 1792–1750 BCE. During his reign he extended his control across southern Mesopotamia.

His expansion also brought destruction to the palace of Zimri-Lim at Mari. The tablets recovered there offer another view of this world: the administration and correspondence of a neighbouring kingdom.
