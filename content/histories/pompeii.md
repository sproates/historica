Pompeii’s excavated streets bring houses, shops, baths and theatres into the same view. They let us follow activities that often disappear behind accounts of emperors and wars.

The eruption of Vesuvius buried the city in 79 CE. Its remains preserve evidence from different phases of the city’s life, not a record of every inhabitant.
