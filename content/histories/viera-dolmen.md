Viera has a long passage leading into a burial chamber. Upright stones and roof slabs form its internal spaces, divided by doorways.

The 2016 ICOMOS assessment places construction in the last third of the fourth millennium BCE. It also reports later activity, possibly funerary or religious, in the Copper and Bronze Ages. The construction window shown here does not cover all that later use.
