Newgrange, Knowth and Dowth were built around 3200 BCE in the Boyne Valley in Ireland. These passage tombs form part of the wider Brú na Bóinne landscape.

Their carved stones preserve a substantial collection of megalithic art. The date shown marks their approximate construction, not the end of their use or significance.
