Athens sent a major expedition to Sicily in 415 BCE. Alcibiades, Nicias and Lamachus shared the initial command.

Alcibiades was recalled, and the remaining commanders pursued the campaign against Syracuse. Despite reinforcements, the expedition ended in disaster in 413 BCE. Many survivors became prisoners.
