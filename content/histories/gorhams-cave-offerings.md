The pottery left at Gorham’s Cave connects the sanctuary with places on both sides of the Mediterranean. The Gibraltar National Museum identifies vessels from Carthage, the Tangier peninsula, Gadir and workshops in what is now Andalusia, alongside material from the eastern Mediterranean.

Pottery shapes and clays help researchers work out where objects were made. Their presence in Gibraltar records the movement of goods, but does not prove that each object arrived directly from its place of manufacture, or that its donor came from there. The sanctuary preserves connections across the Strait without supplying a complete map of the journeys involved.
