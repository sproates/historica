Khufu ruled Egypt during the Fourth Dynasty, succeeding his father Snefru. His tomb, the Great Pyramid at Giza, is the largest pyramid in Egypt.

His father had already commissioned three enormous pyramids and developed their smooth-sided form. Khufu’s pyramid continued that work, as part of a complex with temples, a causeway and nearby cemeteries for officials.

Power was concentrated around the king, with senior offices often held by his relatives. The state planned the cemeteries around Khufu’s pyramid too, placing officials close to the king in death.
