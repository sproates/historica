Born in 63 BCE, Octavian became Caesar’s heir after the assassination in 44 BCE. He was still a young man when he entered the struggle for power that followed.

Victory over Antony and Cleopatra at Actium removed his principal rivals. In 27 BCE, the Senate granted him the title Augustus. He died in 14 CE; his lifespan and his years as emperor are shown separately.
