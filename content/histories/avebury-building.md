Avebury’s banks, ditches and stone circles were built and altered over centuries, roughly between 2850 and 2200 BCE. It was a ceremonial complex rather than a single construction project.

The order in which its features appeared is still being investigated. That matters when comparing Avebury with Stonehenge: both changed over time, and their surviving forms combine different stages.
