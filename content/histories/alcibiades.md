Alcibiades was an Athenian politician and commander whose career crossed the divisions of the Peloponnesian War. Brought up under Pericles’ guardianship, he became closely associated with Socrates.

He helped persuade Athens to send its great expedition to Sicily, then fled to Sparta after being recalled for trial. His later return to Athenian service makes his life a useful way through the war’s changing alliances.
