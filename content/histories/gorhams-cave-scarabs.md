Among the offerings at Gorham’s Cave are small scarabs with engraved designs. The Gibraltar National Museum identifies two illustrated examples as Egyptian, bearing hieroglyphs, and assigns them to the shrine’s earlier phases, between the ninth and sixth centuries BCE.

Other scarabs from the cave imitate Egyptian forms but were made elsewhere. The museum describes these as later examples. An Egyptian-looking object therefore needs closer examination before we can say where it came from. Neither group tells us, by itself, who carried it to Gibraltar.
