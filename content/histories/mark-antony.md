Mark Antony fought for Caesar and became one of the central figures in the struggle after his death. He and Octavian were allies before becoming rivals.

His alliance with Cleopatra tied his future to Ptolemaic Egypt. Octavian defeated their forces at Actium in 31 BCE. Antony died the following year.
