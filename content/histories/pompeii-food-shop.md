At the shop associated with Vetutius Placidus, customers could buy hot food and drinks from a masonry counter fitted with large jars. The house behind it was connected to the business.

A shrine on the back wall included household protectors and the gods Mercury and Dionysus. Commerce, domestic space and religious practice met in one building.
