Excavations at Harappa have produced sets of weights in graduated sizes. Their regular proportions show a shared system of measurement. The excavators suggest that they may have helped regulate trade; collecting taxes is a further possibility, rather than an established fact.

Harappa and Mohenjo-daro also had trading connections to the west. The weights give this wider story a practical dimension, but they cannot identify the goods weighed in a particular transaction or reconstruct a merchant’s route.
