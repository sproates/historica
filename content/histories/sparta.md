Sparta led the coalition fighting Athens in the Peloponnesian War. Its victory in 404 BCE forced Athens to give up its empire.

Alcibiades’ connection takes you inside that rivalry: after leaving Athenian service, he sought refuge with the Spartans and advised them against his own city.
