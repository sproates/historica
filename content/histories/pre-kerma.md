Before the kingdom of Kerma, communities along the Nile developed a large enclosed settlement in Upper Nubia. The archaeological mission places this Pre-Kerma phase around 3500–2500 BCE.

The enclosure suggests concern with protecting people and their resources. Evidence from the wider region is limited, so the extent of connections with neighbouring communities is harder to reconstruct.
