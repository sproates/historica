Liangzhu developed in the lower Yangtze region around 3300–2300 BCE. Rice cultivation supported a society whose remains include a planned city, large earthworks and systems for managing water.

Differences between burials also show differences in status. This gives us another early urban history to place beside Egypt and Mesopotamia, without assuming that their societies followed the same pattern.
