Mari stood at Tell Hariri in the Syrian Euphrates valley. This view follows the city's third major phase, ending around the time Hammurabi's forces destroyed its royal palace.

Excavators recovered thousands of tablets from the palace. They include administrative records and royal correspondence, giving us evidence of the kingdom's affairs as well as its buildings.
