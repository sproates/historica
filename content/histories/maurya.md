The Mauryan dynasty brought much of the Indian subcontinent under one ruling house. Chandragupta founded it, followed by Bindusara and Ashoka.

Its capital was Pataliputra. The connections here follow the rulers, their dealings with Alexander’s successors and the surviving evidence of Ashoka’s reign.
