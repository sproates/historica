Later farmers at Kuk used ditches to cultivate wet ground. The research dates the beginning of this phase to about 2400–2030 BCE.

This follows the much earlier evidence for cultivation on mounds. The two entries separate changes in farming practice rather than treating thousands of years of activity as one event.
