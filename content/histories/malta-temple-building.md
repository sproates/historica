Communities on Malta and Gozo built substantial stone temples during the fourth and third millennia BCE. The surviving complexes differ in their plans and decoration.

Builders combined upright slabs with horizontal stonework, creating enclosed spaces approached through forecourts. The broad timeline window brings these building traditions into view; it does not date every temple to the same phase.
