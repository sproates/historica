Cleopatra VII lived from 69 to 30 BCE and was the last queen of the Ptolemaic dynasty. Her position joined the politics of Egypt to the struggles for power in Rome.

Her relationships with Caesar and then Mark Antony were also political alliances. In Egypt, she drew on established traditions of royal authority while adapting them to her own circumstances.
