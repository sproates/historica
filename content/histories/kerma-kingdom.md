Kerma was the centre of a Nubian kingdom south of Egypt, near the Nile’s Third Cataract. Its capital and eastern cemetery preserve evidence spanning roughly 2500–1500 BCE.

These sites provide a history of political power and burial traditions in Nubia itself. Explore the earlier Pre-Kerma communities to see the longer history of settlement in the area.
