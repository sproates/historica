Seleucus served under Alexander and later established a kingdom of his own. He sought refuge with Ptolemy in Egypt before recovering power in Babylonia.

His settlement with Chandragupta connects the struggles among Alexander’s successors with the rise of the Mauryan state. He was assassinated in 281 BCE.
