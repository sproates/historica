Some of Lahun’s papyri concern very practical matters: lists of workers, grain accounts, animal produce and trips to count fields. They show the record-keeping behind the town’s activities.

The surviving documents come from different groups. Business papers for the cult of Senusret II date to the reigns of Senwosret III and Amenemhat III. Other manuscripts recovered across the town belong to the late Twelfth and early Thirteenth Dynasties. They should not be read as one archive written at a single moment.
