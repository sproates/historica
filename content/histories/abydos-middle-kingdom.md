Abydos drew people through its association with Osiris. In the Middle Kingdom, a much older royal tomb was regarded as the god’s burial place. Annual processions travelled from the town’s temple to the early royal cemetery in the desert.

Officials could establish a presence there through chapels and commemorative stelae along the religious route. Senwosret III also had a tomb, temple and town at Abydos. The site therefore brings together royal construction, religious observance and personal commemoration.
