Nicias was an Athenian commander and politician who favoured peace with Sparta. The treaty negotiated in 421 BCE bears his name.

He opposed the proposed expedition to Sicily but nevertheless became one of its commanders alongside Alcibiades and Lamachus. His career ended with the expedition’s defeat in 413 BCE.
