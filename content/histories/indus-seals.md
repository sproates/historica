A seal from Mohenjo-daro, catalogued as HR 743, has eight signs above a one-horned animal. It was made from glazed steatite and has a perforated projection on its reverse.

The inscription is visible, but visibility is not the same as understanding. The Indus writing system remains undeciphered, so assigning a personal name, title or translation to this example would go beyond the evidence. Its material, image and findspot are firmer starting points.
