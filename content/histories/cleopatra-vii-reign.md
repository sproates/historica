Cleopatra ruled Egypt from 51 to 30 BCE. She belonged to the dynasty established by Ptolemy I, one of Alexander’s successors.

Her reign brought that long history into direct contact with Caesar, Antony and Octavian. The links here lead back into Ptolemaic Egypt and forward to Roman rule.
