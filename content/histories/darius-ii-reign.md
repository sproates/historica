Darius II ruled the Persian Empire during the later years of the Peloponnesian War. His officials negotiated with Sparta, and Persian money helped support its fleet.

This places the war within a wider struggle over influence in the Aegean and western Anatolia. Persia was an active participant, not just a distant background.
