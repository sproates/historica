In the first century BCE, elaborate stone gateways were added to the Great Stupa at Sanchi. The Archaeological Survey of India associates this work with the Andhra-Satavahanas.

The carvings tell stories from the Buddha’s life and the Jatakas. Alongside the wars around the Mediterranean, the timeline shows a community investing in a Buddhist sanctuary with a much longer history.
