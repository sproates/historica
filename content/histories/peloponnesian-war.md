The Peloponnesian War set Athens and its allies against Sparta and its coalition. It ran from 431 to 404 BCE, with a negotiated peace interrupting the main conflict.

Fighting extended well beyond the two cities. The Sicilian expedition and Persian involvement are part of the same history. Athens eventually surrendered and lost its empire.
