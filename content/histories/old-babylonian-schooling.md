House F at Nippur is widely interpreted as a scribal school. Curator Susanne Paulus places it around 1740 BCE.

The tablets found there document an educational curriculum. They let us explore learning alongside the reigns and wars of the Old Babylonian period, while the identification of the building as a school remains an archaeological interpretation.
