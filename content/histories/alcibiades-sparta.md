Recalled from Sicily for trial in 415 BCE, Alcibiades escaped to Sparta. He advised Athens’ enemies as the war widened.

By 412 BCE he was helping Spartan operations in Ionia. This was a changing political allegiance, not an uninterrupted stay in the city of Sparta.
