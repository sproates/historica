Senwosret III, also written Senusret III, was a king of Egypt’s Twelfth Dynasty. His campaigns in Nubia were accompanied by a chain of forts around Semna at the Second Cataract. This was a frontier of Egyptian military power, rather than a boundary around all the societies further south.

He also established a tomb complex at Abydos, with a temple and an associated town. The map uses a general Abydos reference to locate that connection. It does not trace his movements or identify where he lived.
