Dholavira stood on Khadir island in present-day Gujarat. Its remains include a walled settlement, a cemetery, residential areas and workshops. Stone was used extensively in its construction.

UNESCO places its occupation at roughly 3000–1500 BCE, covering a longer span than the urban-phase window used for the wider Indus overview. The city changed over those centuries. Its water systems deserve a closer look alongside its buildings.
