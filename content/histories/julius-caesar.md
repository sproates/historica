Julius Caesar lived from 100 to 44 BCE, during the final decades of the Roman Republic. His rise ended in dictatorship and assassination.

His death did not restore political stability. His heir Octavian, later known as Augustus, eventually emerged as Rome’s first emperor. The connections below follow the people and conflicts that link Caesar’s lifetime to that change.
