Stonehenge developed over many generations. The first earthwork was built around 3000 BCE; the main stone settings followed around 2500 BCE.

The early monument was also a burial place. Later changes included rearranging the bluestones and creating the Avenue towards the River Avon. The date range here covers its principal prehistoric building phases, not its entire history.
