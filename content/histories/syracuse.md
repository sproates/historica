Syracuse was the principal target of the Athenian expedition to Sicily. Its defenders, aided by the Spartan commander Gylippus, resisted the siege.

The Athenian expedition was destroyed in 413 BCE. Syracuse’s connections show this part of the war from the city being attacked, rather than only from Athens.
