At Watson Brake in present-day Louisiana, people built a complex of eleven earthen mounds and connecting ridges. Construction developed in episodes, beginning with smaller earthworks around 3500 BCE.

Food remains indicate a varied diet including nuts, fish and deer. Large communal earthworks were part of this foraging society, well before the later mound complex at Poverty Point.
