Harappa’s excavations preserve evidence of shell working, pottery and bead making. Craftspeople worked agate and steatite, including glazed steatite beads, and used different drilling techniques.

Jonathan Mark Kenoyer’s research traces several of these crafts from the site’s early levels through its later prehistoric occupation. The urban period shown here is one part of that longer history. Looking at production helps us see the objects as things people made, rather than simply pieces found in a museum.
