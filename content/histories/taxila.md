Taxila preserves several successive settlements. The Bhir mound contains the remains of an early historic town; Sirkap and Sirsukh belong to later phases.

Its position brought it into contact with Persia, the Greek world and Central Asia. Buddhist monasteries and monuments were part of its changing urban landscape.
