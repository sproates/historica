An oligarchic coup replaced Athenian democracy with the government of the Four Hundred in 411 BCE. Hopes of obtaining Persian support formed part of the political manoeuvring.

Alcibiades was involved in proposals linking a change of government to his own return. The new regime was divided from the start.
