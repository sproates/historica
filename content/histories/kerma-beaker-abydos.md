A thin-walled Kerma beaker was found in Tomb 525 at Abydos, in Egypt. The Met dates it to roughly 1802–1640 BCE. Its red body and black top required careful firing, after the pot had been shaped by hand and polished.

Kerma pottery has been found with Egyptian goods in several Egyptian tombs. The Met suggests that some owners may have been traders from Kerma living in Egypt, but their identities are uncertain. The beaker is evidence of a connection between the two regions; it does not tell us the route it travelled or who carried it.
