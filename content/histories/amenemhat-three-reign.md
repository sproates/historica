Amenemhat III ruled during Egypt’s Twelfth Dynasty. His building projects included two pyramids: one at Dahshur and another at Hawara, near the entrance to the Fayum. Ground problems at Dahshur led to the choice of a second site.

His interest in the Fayum also appears in buildings at Medinet Maadi and the colossi at Biahmu. At Lahun, temple accounts dated to his reign offer a different view of the period, through the administration behind a royal cult.
