Yangshao communities belong to Neolithic China, approximately 5000–3000 BCE. Their surviving objects include painted pottery, stone tools and equipment for working textiles.

The National Museum of China’s collection includes knives, a stone mortar, spindle whorls and bone needles. Together they offer a view of practical work and decorated objects, long before the later dynasties.
