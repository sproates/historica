Harappa is one of the cities that gives the Indus civilisation its other name, Harappan. Its excavated sequence extends beyond the urban phase shown here, which runs from roughly 2600 to 1900 BCE.

The remains are less visually complete than those at Mohenjo-daro. Brick removal left fragmentary walls and mounds, but the architecture and objects still show their shared cultural tradition. Evidence of shell working, pottery and bead production gives us another way to explore the city, through the work carried out there.
