Two seasonal streams supplied Dholavira in an arid setting. Reservoirs lay east and south of the citadel, alongside infrastructure for managing and draining water.

The displayed dates follow the city’s broad occupation history. They do not mean that every reservoir was built together or that the same system operated unchanged for fifteen centuries.
