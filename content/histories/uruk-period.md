During the Uruk period, cities developed around large mud-brick temples. The Met places this period at approximately 4000–3100 BCE.

Growing administration changed how goods and transactions were recorded. Clay tablets and cylinder seals became part of this changing urban society. The separate Uruk city entry lets you explore the later part of this period more closely.
