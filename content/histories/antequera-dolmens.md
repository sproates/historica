At Antequera, three prehistoric monuments preserve different ways of building with stone. Menga and Viera have chambers roofed with great slabs; El Romeral uses corbelled chambers, where successive courses project inwards to close the roof. Earth mounds cover all three.

Their setting matters too. Menga faces La Peña de los Enamorados, while El Romeral is aligned towards El Torcal. The monuments formed part of a landscape used for burial and ritual, rather than standing apart from it.
