Ubaid communities farmed with irrigation and fished the rivers and Gulf. Between about 5500 and 4000 BCE, related pottery traditions spread across much of Mesopotamia.

Some villages grew into towns centred on large buildings, including at Eridu and Uruk. These developments preceded the better-known cities of the Uruk period.
