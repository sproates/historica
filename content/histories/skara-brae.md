Skara Brae was a village in Orkney occupied around 3100–2500 BCE. Its stone houses preserve details of domestic life, including hearths, beds and storage furniture.

People farmed, hunted and fished, and made pottery, tools and jewellery. The surviving rooms bring ordinary households into view alongside the monuments and royal tombs elsewhere on the timeline.
