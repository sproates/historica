A severe epidemic struck Athens during the early years of the war. People had crowded into the city for protection, and the disease killed soldiers and civilians, including Pericles in 429 BCE.

Thucydides survived it and described what he saw. The illness is conventionally called the plague of Athens, but its precise cause remains uncertain.
