Roman rule began in Egypt in 30 BCE, after Cleopatra’s death. The Ptolemaic dynasty, established by Ptolemy I in 305 BCE, had ended.

This is the point where the Ptolemaic and Roman Egypt entries meet. It marks a change in government, not an end to Egyptian society or its traditions.
