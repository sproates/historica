Cicero was a Roman politician, lawyer and philosophical writer. His speeches and letters give us a particularly close view of the Republic’s final decades.

As consul in 63 BCE, he suppressed Catiline’s conspiracy. He later watched the struggle between Caesar and Pompey turn into civil war. Caesar’s assassination brought only a brief respite: Cicero was killed on Mark Antony’s orders in 43 BCE.
