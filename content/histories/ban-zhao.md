Ban Zhao was a scholar and teacher at the Eastern Han court. She helped complete the history of the Western Han after her brother Ban Gu died.

Her writing also addressed women’s education and conduct. It gives us a named woman’s argument about family life, rather than a neutral account of how every household worked.
