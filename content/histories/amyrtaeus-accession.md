Around 404 BCE, Amyrtaeus established himself as an Egyptian ruler and began consolidating independence from Persia. He was the sole king of the Twenty-eighth Dynasty.

The change did not happen everywhere at once. Documents from Elephantine still recognised the Persian king as late as 401 BCE. This is a gradual political transition, despite the single date on the timeline.
