Nippur lay in the Middle Euphrates region of present-day Iraq. It was a centre of worship of the god Enlil during the third and second millennia BCE.

The city also gives us evidence of education. House F, widely interpreted as a scribal school, is the focus of the linked account of learning around 1740 BCE.
