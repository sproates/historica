Gorham’s Cave gives us a way into Gibraltar’s ancient history. At the foot of the Rock, a coastal sanctuary received offerings from people travelling through the Strait. The upper archaeological level is associated with Phoenician and Carthaginian use between the ninth and third centuries BCE.

Start with the sanctuary, then explore its scarabs and pottery. These are selected finds from one site, rather than a complete history of Gibraltar.
