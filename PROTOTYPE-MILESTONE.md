# Prototype finish line and next phase

Agreed with Ian on 23 November 2026.

The prototype is complete when these three additions have been checked on the persistent local preview, approved and published:

1. Select a year within the visible timeline without losing its surrounding period. Show year-level age estimates and dated map associations. Preserve the selected year in bookmarks.
2. Choose any two people for a full-lifespan comparison, including non-overlapping lives. Preserve the comparison in bookmarks.
3. Distinguish lifespans, reigns, events, approximate dates, archaeological snapshots and editorial study windows. Do not imply exact ages or continuous activity where the evidence does not support them.

Ian approved publication of all three features and the fitted initial map on 23 November 2026. Published and verified on historica.online. The prototype milestone is complete. This milestone does not require completing worldwide data coverage or historical boundary geometry.

## Following phase: sustainable application

After accepting the prototype, change direction from incremental prototype expansion to a maintainable application. Candidate work agreed for this phase:

- Review repository contents and history before making it public, including old deployment information and licensing for data and assets.
- Introduce GitHub CI for builds, data validation and relevant tests.
- Move towards complete static page generation. Current pages contain static metadata and introductory content, while much of the experience is rendered in the browser.
- Evaluate GitHub Pages and the domain/deployment migration, including support for existing paths and bookmarks.
- Add proportionate analytics, with privacy and consent choices decided explicitly.
- Refactor application state, rendering and data processing into maintainable components, preserving the accepted prototype behaviour.

These are the next phase’s intended direction, not instructions to enable services now. The repository remains private; builds remain manual; ASO remains the production host until the relevant migration is agreed. Design and interaction changes still require preview approval before publication.
