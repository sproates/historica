Map credits and collection scope

This collection covers 15 main stages from Alexander’s accession in 336 BCE to his death in 323 BCE. Four background records cover China, Italy and Monte Albán. Markers use rounded reference positions; regional markers do not identify capitals or borders. The campaign lines are schematic links, not reconstructed travel routes or conquered territory. Empty areas indicate gaps in coverage. Ages are year-level estimates from a birth year of 356 BCE, not exact ages at each event.

Made with Natural Earth (public domain). Contemporary borders are geographical reference data, not a reconstruction of ancient political boundaries.

The Bactria overlay is an original, approximate editorial sketch (CC0), informed by the Barrington Atlas commentary and Encyclopaedia Iranica. It omits northern Bactria and does not claim a dated political frontier.

Bactra’s approximate position is rounded from Pleiades 961886, by St J. Simpson, F. Hiebert, P. L. Kohl and contributors, CC BY 3.0. The location records credit OpenStreetMap contributors. This is a city reference point, not a personal location observation.
